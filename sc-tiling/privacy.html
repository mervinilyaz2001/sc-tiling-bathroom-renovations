Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

SC Tiling & Bathroom Renovations ("we", "us") provides bathroom renovation, wet room, and kitchen & floor tiling services in Galway and the surrounding areas. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the name, phone number, and email address you enter, along with anything you tell us about your project. If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp instead, we see whatever details you share with us there.

This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies, and we don't collect any information automatically just from you browsing the site.

Why we collect it

We use your details solely to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation, and provide a quote. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties except where needed to carry out work you've asked us to do (for example, coordinating a delivery to your address).

How we store it

Enquiries submitted through the website are sent to our email inbox. Enquiries made by phone or WhatsApp are kept on our business phone. We don't maintain a separate marketing database.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead with us, for the duration of your project and for standard bookkeeping afterward. After that, we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll sort it out promptly.

Cookies

This website currently doesn't set any tracking or analytics cookies. If that changes in future, we'll ask for your consent through the cookie banner before anything is set, and update this page to explain what's used and why.

Links to other sites

Our WhatsApp link takes you to a service run by Meta. Once you leave our site, their own privacy policy applies, not ours.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
